Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions before getting started.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most Starter and Business sites are delivered in 3–7 days depending on content and revisions. Professional/custom projects may take longer.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Ideally yes — your logo, text and photos. If you don't have them ready, I can help source stock images and write basic content.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes, every website I build is fully mobile responsive and tested across devices.

Do you provide hosting and domain setup?

Yes, I can help set up hosting and connect your custom domain, or guide you through it if you prefer to manage it yourself.

What if I need changes after the website is live?

Minor revisions are included right after delivery. Ongoing updates or new features can be handled as a separate small project.
